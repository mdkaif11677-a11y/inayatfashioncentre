Since day one

A shop run
on a promise.

Inaayat Fashion Centre began as a small counter of carefully chosen cloth on Dargah Road — and grew on word of mouth, repeat customers, and a proprietor who still measures every metre himself.

Today the shop carries everyday cottons, wedding silks, embroidered suits and shawls for every season, sourced with the same attention to quality it opened with.
